THE CONCEPT

The room is the accelerator.

At every winecoding event, builders present a real project — an MVP, an AI tool, a side project. Then the room takes over.

Developers help with architecture. Designers rethink UX. AI builders automate workflows. Founders stress-test strategy.

Weeks of progress compressed into a single evening.

WHAT YOU GET

Real skills from
real builders.

FOR VIBECODING

Navigate the traps.

AI-generated code ships fast but breaks faster. Get real-time feedback on architecture, prompt strategies, and the blind spots that vibecoding tools won't warn you about.

FOR SOLO BUILDERS

Marketing from people who ship.

You can code it, but can you sell it? Growth marketers and product people in the room help you with positioning, launch strategy, and the messaging that actually converts.

FOR SECURITY

Code review that matters.

Experienced engineers review your architecture, flag security risks, and catch the vulnerabilities that AI-generated code quietly introduces. Honest audits, no invoices.

FOR FOUNDERS

Business strategy, unfiltered.

Pricing, fundraising, GTM, hiring — founders who've been through it share what actually worked. No theoretical frameworks, just battle-tested advice over a glass of wine.

FOR EVERYONE

Tools, stacks, workflows.

Members share the tools and setups they actually use — not sponsored listicles. From dev environments to AI agents to deployment pipelines, curated by people who build daily.

FOR DESIGNERS

Ship, don't just mock up.

Developers in the room help designers go from Figma to production. Learn to prototype with code, integrate AI tools, and build things that actually run — not just look good.
